# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own system architecture decisions and ensure technical consistency across the project. They provide technical guidance to the team and translate between technical and business concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Own and review system architecture decisions
- Ensure technical consistency and quality standards across the codebase
- Mentor Developers and guide technical problem-solving
- Advise Product Managers and Project Managers on technical trade-offs and effort estimates
- Coordinate with Platform/SRE teams for operability and scalability concerns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies

### Goals
- Enable scalable, maintainable system design
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build a technically strong team through mentorship
- Align architecture with business objectives

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- One-on-ones with team members on skill development
- Cross-team coordination on technical dependencies
- Escalations to engineering leadership on major decisions

---

## Engineering Manager

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers lead people, manage team capacity, and support career development. They enable the delivery team to perform effectively and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Hire, onboard, and develop engineers
- Manage capacity planning and workload distribution
- Conduct performance reviews and career development conversations
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate appropriately
- Foster psychological safety and continuous learning
- Collaborate with Product and Project Managers on resourcing and timelines

### Goals
- Build high-performing, engaged teams
- Reduce turnover and improve retention
- Accelerate team velocity through effective capacity planning
- Develop future leaders

### Typical Communication
- 1:1s with direct reports
- Team retrospectives and feedback sessions
- Weekly syncs with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Escalations to HR or senior leadership when needed

---

## UX Researcher / Product Designer

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Product Designers lead discovery, define user-centered design solutions, and ensure accessibility. They bridge customer needs and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and synthesize customer insights
- Create design specifications and prototypes
- Ensure design accessibility and usability standards
- Partner with Product Managers to define acceptance criteria
- Validate design decisions with user feedback
- Support Developers during implementation with design guidance

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, user-centric solutions
- Reduce rework due to design misalignment
- Improve customer satisfaction and adoption
- Ensure accessibility compliance

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Design specs and prototypes in PR reviews
- Collaboration on acceptance criteria with Product Managers

---

## QA Lead / Test Engineer

### Role Summary
QA Leads and Test Engineers own test strategy, coordinate quality assurance activities, and establish sign-off criteria. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test case ownership
- Coordinate manual and automated testing efforts
- Establish quality metrics and acceptance sign-off criteria
- Work with Developers to improve testability
- Collaborate with Security and Compliance teams on compliance testing
- Execute release readiness verification

### Goals
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Reduce testing cycle time through automation
- Establish clear, measurable quality standards
- Support rapid, confident releases

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and case documentation
- Bug reports and quality metrics dashboards
- Testability discussions with Developers
- Release readiness sign-off with Project Managers

---

## Release / Platform Engineer

### Role Summary
Release and Platform Engineers maintain deployment infrastructure, automate release processes, and ensure smooth, reliable deployments to production.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain deployment pipelines and automation
- Create and maintain runbooks and operational procedures
- Manage rollback procedures and disaster recovery
- Coordinate with Developers and SRE during releases
- Perform smoke tests and post-deployment verifications
- Collaborate with Project Managers on deployment scheduling

### Goals
- Enable fast, reliable releases with minimal manual effort
- Reduce deployment failures and rollbacks
- Improve observability and incident response
- Automate repetitive release tasks

### Typical Communication
- Deployment automation and pipeline documentation
- Runbook reviews and updates
- Coordination on deployment windows with Project Managers
- On-call rotations and incident response

---

## Data Analyst / Metrics Owner

### Role Summary
Data Analysts and Metrics Owners define success metrics, build dashboards, and analyze experiment results to inform product and business decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define measurable success metrics aligned with business objectives
- Build dashboards and monitoring for key performance indicators
- Analyze experiment results and A/B test outcomes
- Work with Product Managers to ensure instrumentation is in place
- Provide data-driven recommendations for prioritization
- Document analysis methodology and findings

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making
- Demonstrate product impact through metrics
- Reduce guesswork in prioritization
- Accelerate learning cycles through experimentation

### Typical Communication
- Metrics definitions and dashboard design
- Experiment analysis and findings presentations
- Weekly or bi-weekly sync with Product Managers
- Technical discussions with Developers on instrumentation

---

## Customer Success / Support Liaison

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Liaisons bridge the gap between customers and the product team. They gather customer feedback, prioritize fixes, and own post-release communications.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and synthesize customer feedback and support requests
- Prioritize bug fixes and feature requests based on customer impact
- Participate in release planning to ensure customer-critical items are included
- Own post-release communications and customer announcements
- Support QA in reproducing and validating customer-reported issues
- Identify trends in customer issues and escalate to Product Managers

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce time to resolution for customer issues
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product decisions
- Build strong customer relationships

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and trend reports
- Support requests and bug prioritization
- Release announcements and customer communications
- Collaboration with QA on issue reproduction and validation

---

## Security / Compliance Representative

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Representatives ensure products meet security and compliance requirements. They review designs, coordinate security scans, and remediate vulnerabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Review product designs for security and compliance impact
- Approve security-related architectural decisions
- Coordinate security scanning and vulnerability management
- Guide Developers on secure coding practices
- Collaborate with Compliance teams on regulatory requirements
- Participate in incident response for security issues

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities and compliance violations
- Build security awareness across the team
- Maintain customer trust through strong security practices
- Ensure regulatory compliance

### Typical Communication
- Security design reviews and threat modeling
- Vulnerability reports and remediation plans
- Security training and best practices guidance
- Compliance audit support and documentation
